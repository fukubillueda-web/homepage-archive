The Breuil–Mézard conjecture for non-scalar split residual representations

with Yongquan Hu · Annales Scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure 48 (2015), no. 6, 1383–1421

Proves the Breuil–Mézard conjecture for split non-scalar residual representations of the local Galois group by local methods; together with earlier cases this completes the conjecture for p ≥ 5. As a consequence, it removes the remaining local restriction in Kisin’s proof of the Fontaine–Mazur conjecture, yielding the corresponding two-dimensional modularity theorem.

arXiv / full text · journal
